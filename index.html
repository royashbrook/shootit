How to play

  1. Slide your finger to steer your crowd of buddies.
  2. Run through a gate to do its maths: ×2 makes your crowd bigger, −5 makes it smaller.
  3. Your buddies shoot stars at slimes all by themselves.
  4. Pop the big boss slime at the end to win!

If all your buddies pop, just tap TRY AGAIN — nothing is lost, ever. Take as long as you want.
